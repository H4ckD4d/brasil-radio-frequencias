# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

RadioSync is committed to a welcoming, technically rigorous community. Participation must remain respectful regardless of experience, identity, location, language, license class, equipment, or technical preference.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence and technical decisions without attacking people.
- Be patient with new contributors and explain requested changes.
- Credit other people's work accurately.
- Respect privacy, source licenses, embargoes, and lawful-use boundaries.
- Correct mistakes openly and preserve the review history.
- Keep safety-sensitive discussion focused on legitimate documentation and authorized use.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or sexualized conduct.
- Publishing private information, credentials, or private communications.
- Knowingly submitting fabricated data or false verification claims.
- Removing attribution or presenting another person's work as one's own.
- Encouraging unlawful transmission, interference, unauthorized access, or evasion of regulation.
- Repeated disruption of reviews or community spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, lock, or remove contributions and participation that violate this policy. Responses should be proportionate and may include a private warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on [h4ckd4d's GitHub profile](https://github.com/H4ckD4d). Do not include sensitive details in a public Issue. Maintainers should protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible and disclose conflicts of interest.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents RadioSync in public spaces.
